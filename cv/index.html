Research Programs

  • 2026 - Present
    Agentic Deep Search and Iterative Retrieval
    Agentic systems for complex question answering through iterative search, reading, and reasoning.
    • Question’s Gambit achieved 90.5% answer accuracy on BrowseComp-Plus, ranking first in recall (96.6%) and the only academic submission among the leaderboard’s leaders.
    • ADORE uses retrieval-grounded relevance feedback for query expansion and improved average nDCG@10 by 122.9% over BM25 on BRIGHT.
  • 2026 - Present
    Open Research Infrastructure for Agentic Retrieval
    Open tools for building, reproducing, and evaluating retrieval pipelines.
    • Built and publicly released TRAWL, a visual platform for composing, testing, and evaluating agentic retrieval flows.
    • Co-developed QueryGym (The Web Conference Companion 2026), a toolkit for reproducible LLM-based query reformulation, and contributed to a SIGIR 2026 reproducibility study of reformulation methods.
  • 2025 - Present
    LLM Safety and Adversarial Robustness
    Failure modes of aligned LLMs and retrieval systems through prompt-embedding optimization, adversarial content injection against neural rankers, graph poisoning, and adversarial edge perturbation.
    • Combines attacks, evaluation resources, and evidence for robust system design.
  • 2023 - 2025
    WISEST: Human-Centred Evidence Synthesis
    Led AI development for an internally deployed system that supports structured, explainable assessment of systematic reviews.
    • Collaboration between the University of Toronto, the University of British Columbia, and Unity Health Toronto; contributed to a peer-reviewed article in Research Synthesis Methods.
  • 2019 - 2025
    Graph Representation Learning and Team Formation
    Neural representations and retrieval methods for complete and incomplete heterogeneous graphs, with applications to subgraph search, expert-team formation, and recommendation.
    • Released research software including ROKSANA, PyTFL, and PyDHNet.
