### Overview

Progress in retrieval research depends on being able to build, compare, and reproduce pipelines easily. This program releases open tools for that.

### Systems

- **TRAWL**: a publicly released visual platform for composing, testing, and evaluating agentic retrieval flows.
- **QueryGym**: a toolkit for reproducible LLM-based query reformulation, presented at The Web Conference Companion 2026. [Website](https://ls3-lab.github.io/QueryGym/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/ls3-lab/QueryGym)
- A SIGIR 2026 reproducibility study of LLM-based query reformulation methods, built on QueryGym.
