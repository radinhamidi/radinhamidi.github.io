### Overview

WISEST (WhIch Systematic Evidence Synthesis is besT) is an interdisciplinary initiative focused on developing AI tools to enhance the quality assessment of systematic reviews (2023-2025). I led the AI development stream, designing a cutting-edge Generative AI model capable of performing comprehensive analyses of systematic-review research papers while providing robust, transparent explanations of each decision.

### Key Contributions

- Designed and implemented the end-to-end AI pipeline, from data ingestion and preprocessing to model deployment.
- Developed explainability modules to ensure the model’s recommendations remain interpretable and trustworthy for domain experts.
- Coordinated cross-functional efforts between clinical researchers, knowledge-translation experts, and software engineers.

### Collaborators

- Knowledge Translation Program, Unity Health Toronto (St. Michael’s Hospital)
- University of British Columbia
- University of Toronto

### Outputs

- **WISEST System**: an internally deployed system that supports structured, explainable assessment of systematic reviews.
- **WISEST Dataset Challenge** – open benchmark dataset released to foster further research.
- **WISEST Annotation Platform** – custom interface used by domain experts to curate high-quality training data.
