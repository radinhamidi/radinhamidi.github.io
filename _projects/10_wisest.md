### Overview
WISEST is an interdisciplinary initiative focused on developing AI tools to enhance the quality assessment of systematic reviews (2023-2025). I led the AI development stream, designing a cutting-edge Generative AI model capable of performing comprehensive analyses of systematic-review research papers while providing robust, transparent explanations of each decision.

### Key Contributions
* Designed and implemented the end-to-end AI pipeline, from data ingestion and preprocessing to model deployment.
* Developed explainability modules to ensure the model’s recommendations remain interpretable and trustworthy for domain experts.
* Coordinated cross-functional efforts between clinical researchers, knowledge-translation experts, and software engineers.

### Collaborators
* Knowledge Translation Program, Unity Health Network
* St. Michael’s Hospital
* University of British Columbia
* University of Toronto

### Outputs
* **WISEST Web Tool** – public interface for automated quality appraisal of systematic reviews.
* **WISEST Dataset Challenge** – open benchmark dataset released to foster further research.
* **WISEST Annotation Platform** – custom interface used by domain experts to curate high-quality training data.

> The WISEST platform is poised to accelerate evidence-based decision-making by bringing trustworthy, explainable AI to systematic-review quality appraisal.
