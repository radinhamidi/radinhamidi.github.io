### Overview

This program studies how aligned LLMs and retrieval systems fail under adversarial pressure. It combines attacks, evaluation resources, and evidence for robust system design. The work is supported by the CAD 100,000 CAISI Catalyst Grant _Adversarial Robustness in Knowledge Graphs_ (2025–2026).

### Directions

- **Prompt-embedding optimization** for jailbreaking aligned LLMs. [arXiv:2604.24983](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24983)
- **Adversarial content injection** against neural ranking models, and datasets for supervised attacks on neural rankers.
- **Graph poisoning** to manipulate node rankings.
- **Adversarial edge perturbation** in graph-based retrieval.
