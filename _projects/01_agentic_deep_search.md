### Overview
This program develops agentic systems for complex question answering through iterative search, reading, and reasoning. Instead of answering from a single retrieval step, these agents decide what to search for next, read what they find, and refine their queries until they can answer.

### Highlights
* **Question's Gambit** reached 90.5% answer accuracy on BrowseComp-Plus, ranking first in recall (96.6%). It was the only academic submission among the leaderboard's leaders. [arXiv:2609.14412](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14412)
* **ADORE** uses retrieval-grounded relevance feedback for query expansion and improved average nDCG@10 by 122.9% over BM25 on BRIGHT.
* **Seek** (CIKM 2026): self-evaluative exploration for knowledge retrieval.
* **Route Me If You Can** (CIKM 2026): a benchmark for query reformulation selection.
